How the week works

One shop for the week’s recipes, then whatever sounds good, night by night.

The app knows roughly how long each fresh thing keeps, counts the days since the shop, and puts the recipe whose ingredients are nearest their end at the top. Tap a recipe before you cook and it asks you to check those first.

Pictures: line drawings from the free Food Icon Pack (CC BY 4.0), coloured in.

The red shine means something is waiting on you; the olive wood strip is cropped from your own spatula. Keeping times are typical fridge guidance.
